FAQs

Resource Augmentation Services: Frequently Asked Questions

Resource augmentation (also called staff augmentation or team augmentation) is a flexible hiring model where you bring in external technology professionals who work as part of your team — using your tools, attending your meetings, and reporting to your managers. Unlike outsourcing, augmented resources work under your direction and integrate fully into your workflows. It's the fastest way to scale your team without the overhead of traditional hiring.
With outsourcing, you hand over a project and get deliverables back. With resource augmentation, you get people who join your team and work under your leadership. You maintain full control over priorities, processes, and technical decisions. It's your team, extended — not a vendor relationship.
Typically 5–10 business days for the first placement, depending on role complexity and your interview process. For common roles (full-stack developers, data engineers, DevOps), we can often present candidates within 48 hours because our talent network is continuously curated.
Our standard minimum is 3 months for individual placements and 6 months for dedicated team extensions. Shorter engagements are possible for specific needs — we'll discuss what makes sense during our discovery call.
Every professional in our network goes through a multi-stage assessment: technical screening, live coding or domain exercise, communication and collaboration evaluation, reference verification, and cultural fit assessment. Our acceptance rate is deliberately low to maintain quality.
We offer a replacement guarantee. If a professional isn't meeting expectations — whether technical, communication, or cultural fit — we replace them quickly and at no additional cost during the guarantee period. We'd rather fix a mismatch fast than let it linger.
Rates depend on role seniority, technology specialization, engagement model, and duration. As a general range: mid-level developers start from $4,000–$6,000/month, senior engineers $6,000–$10,000/month, and specialists (ML, cloud architecture, security) $8,000–$15,000/month. We provide detailed, transparent pricing after understanding your requirements.
All augmented professionals sign comprehensive NDAs, IP assignment agreements, and data protection contracts before joining your team. We can work with your legal team to align contracts with your specific requirements.
