Resource Augmentation Services: Frequently Asked Questions

Resource augmentation (also called staff augmentation or IT staff augmentation) is the practice of adding external, pre-vetted specialists to your existing team on demand. Unlike traditional hiring, there's no lengthy recruitment process, no permanent headcount commitment, and no termination cost at the end of a project. You get senior talent working on your project within days — and you scale up or down as your workload demands.
For well-defined requirements, clients typically review a shortlist within a few days and have a vetted specialist working within 48 hours of selection. Average time to first placement is 5–10 business days — dramatically faster than the 6–9 months a permanent hire can take.
Every candidate goes through a rigorous multi-stage process: technical assessments and live coding exercises, system design and architecture interviews, portfolio and project history review, communication and collaboration screening, and, for senior roles, leadership and strategic thinking evaluations. Only the strongest candidates reach your shortlist.
