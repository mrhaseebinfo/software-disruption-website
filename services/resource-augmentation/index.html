Flexible Resource Augmentation Models

Not every team needs the same thing. Choose the model that fits your timeline, budget, and how you like to work.

Individual Specialist Augmentation

  • Minimum engagement: 3 months (flexible extensions)
  • Ideal for targeted skill gaps

Dedicated Team Extension

Best For: Scaling capacity for a major initiative, product build, or platform migration.
  • Multiple resources working as a cohesive unit within your team

  • Full alignment with your sprints, ceremonies, and delivery cadence
  • Minimum engagement: 6 months (flexible scaling)

Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT)

Best For: Organisations that want to build a permanent team but need help getting started.
  • We recruit, onboard, and manage the team initially
  • Full knowledge transfer and operational handover at agreed timeline
  • Transition to your permanent payroll with zero disruption
  • Ideal for setting up offshore or nearshore development centres

On-Demand Talent Pool

Best For: Organisations with fluctuating needs who want pre-vetted talent on standby.
  • Access to a curated pool of pre-vetted professionals
  • Rapid activation when you need additional capacity
  • No commitment until you activate

Resource Augmentation Services: Frequently Asked Questions

Resource augmentation (also called staff augmentation or team augmentation) is a flexible hiring model where you bring in external technology professionals who work as part of your team — using your tools, attending your meetings, and reporting to your managers. Unlike outsourcing, augmented resources work under your direction and integrate fully into your workflows. It's the fastest way to scale your team without the overhead of traditional hiring.
With outsourcing, you hand over a project and get deliverables back. With resource augmentation, you get people who join your team and work under your leadership. You maintain full control over priorities, processes, and technical decisions. It's your team, extended — not a vendor relationship.
